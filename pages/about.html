About Karis Printers

Who We Are

Karis Printers is a leading printing services company based in Nairobi, Kenya. We specialize in delivering high-quality printing solutions for businesses, organizations, and individuals.

With years of experience in the industry, we pride ourselves on providing professional, reliable, and affordable printing services. From business stationery to large-format signage, custom apparel to packaging solutions we handle it all.

Our mission is to make professional printing accessible to everyone through transparent pricing, fast turnaround, and exceptional customer service.

Our Commitment

  • ✅ Quality Guarantee: Premium materials and state-of-the-art equipment
  • ✅ Clear Pricing: No hidden fees. See the price before you order
  • ✅ Fast Turnaround: Most orders completed in 2-3 business days
  • ✅ Free Delivery: On orders over KSh 10,000 within Nairobi
  • ✅ Customer First: Dedicated support throughout the process
